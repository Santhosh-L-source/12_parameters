Verification Checklist
1
Verify student identity matches the certificate.
2
Confirm the issuing organisation is an approved body (JLPT, Goethe, DELF, DELE/SIELE).
3
Check the certificate number or reference code.
4
Confirm the result date is valid and not expired.
5
Map the result to the correct CEFR level (see JLPT table below for score-based mapping).
6
Award is based on highest verified level in ONE non-English language only.
